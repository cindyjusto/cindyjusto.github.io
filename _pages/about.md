Hello!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Sheffield. My research interests are in international trade, development, and labour economics, with a focus on services trade and the digital economy.

My research examines how the international integration of services markets is reshaped by, and reshapes, the firms, workers, and policies operating within these markets. One strand of my work studies how multinationals alter the participation and performance of domestic firms in services trade. A second strand examines how policy and market structure govern services trade and online work, with attention to the regulation of digital trade and to market power in cross-border digital labour markets.
